# UX Research — Multi-Vendor Marketplace App

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** Retail & E-commerce — Multi-Vendor Marketplace App

**Project Type:** UI/UX Design

**Platform:** Mobile Application (iOS and Android)

## 2. Problem Statement

Traditional e-commerce experiences can become difficult to navigate when customers browse products from multiple independent sellers.

Customers need an intuitive way to discover products, evaluate sellers, understand product details, and add products to their cart.

Vendors also need a straightforward way to manage their product listings and inventory.

## 3. Research Objectives

* Understand shopper needs and shopping behavior.
* Identify common product-discovery challenges.
* Understand vendor requirements and inventory-management challenges.
* Identify opportunities to improve marketplace usability and trust.
* Use research insights to guide the Information Architecture and User Flow.

## 4. Target Users

### Shopper

Customers who browse, compare, and purchase products from different sellers.

### Vendor

Independent sellers and D2C businesses that list and manage products on the marketplace.


## 5. Design Implications

Based on the findings, consider improvements such as:

* Clear product categories and search.
* Useful product filters and sorting.
* Visible product and seller information.
* Clear pricing and product options.
* An easy-to-understand cart experience.
* Simple inventory-management workflows for vendors.

Validate these opportunities against the actual research findings.

## 6. Next Steps

* Finalize the Shopper Persona.
* Finalize the Vendor Persona.
* Review the Information Architecture.
* Validate the core User Flow.
* Translate the findings into low-fidelity wireframes.
