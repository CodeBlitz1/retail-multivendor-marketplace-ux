# User Personas

## 1. Shopper Persona

**Created by:** Dolly

### Profile

* Name: Khushi sharma
* Age: 22
* Occupation: collage student
* Shopping behavior: Regularly shops online for cloths, skincare product, accessories, and daily essentials


### Goals

* Find good-quality products at affordable prices
* Shop from multiple sellers in one app.
* Compare prices, ratings, and delivery times easily
* Receive orders on time and return products without difficulty


### Pain Points

* Different sellers offer the same product at different prices.
* Product photos sometimes do not match the actual product
* Delivery charges and extra fees appear at checkout.
* Returns and refunds can take too much time.


### Preferred features

* Smart search
* Price comparison
* Verified reviews
* Wishlist
* Secure payment
* Easy returns
* Order tracking


### Shopping habits

* Mostly shops using her smartphone.
* Compares products and prices before buying.
* Checks customer reviews, ratings, and photos.
* Looks for discounts and free delivery.
* Often shops during sales and festive offers.


### Frustrations

* Unexpected charges appearing at checkout.
* Too many advertisements and irrelevant product recommendations.
* Complicated checkout and payment processes.
* Slow customer support when an order has a problem.
* Delayed refunds and complicated return procedures.


### Device usage

* Smartphone (primary)
* Laptop (secondary)  
* Wi-Fi/mobile data 
* Shopping notifications

## 2. Vendor Persona

**Created by:** Vishwa

### Profile

* Name: Ananya Sharma
* Age: 27
* Occupation: Small business owner
* Business type: Handmade jewellery

### Goals

* Increase sales and reach more customers.
* Manage products and stock easily.
* Keep all orders organized in one place.
* Reduce time spent on manual work.
* Build relationships with repeat customers

### Pain Points

* Stock updates are difficult to manage manually.
* Orders from different platforms get mixed up.
* Finding order details takes too much time.
* Accidentally selling out-of-stock products.
* Managing customer messages while packing orders is stressful.
* Too much time spent on admin work instead of creating products.

### Needs

* An easy way to add and edit products.
* Real-time inventory and stock tracking.
* One place to manage all orders.
* Automatic order status updates and notifications.
* Quick access to customer details and order history.
* A simple, beginner-friendly interface.
* Reliable support when problems occur.
  
### Business type

* Type : Small, independent business
* Products : Handmade jewellery and accessories
* Business size : Small Team
* Sales channels : Instagram, WhatsApp, and marketplace
* Selling style : Made-to-order and ready-to-ship products

### Inventory Problems

* Difficult to keep stock quantities updated.
* Forgets to update inventory after receiving orders.
* Hard to identify products running low on stock.
* Manual stock tracking leads to mistakes.
* Stock information gets mismatched across sales platforms.
* Risk of selling products that are out of stock.

### Customer-Managment Problems

* Difficult to find previous customer conversations.
* Hard to remember special customer requests.
* Managing messages from different platforms is time-consuming.
* Sometimes forgets to follow up with customers.
* Difficult to keep track of customer order history.
* Wants to build better relationships with repeat customers.

### Order-management Problems

* Orders from different platforms are difficult to manage in one place.
* Difficult to track order status and delivery updates.
* Sometimes misses orders or important order details.
* Finding a specific order takes too much time.
* Managing packing and shipping during busy periods is stressful.
* Needs a simple way to track orders from placement to delivery.
